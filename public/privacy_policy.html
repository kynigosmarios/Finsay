Δεν εκτίθενται ποτέ σε άλλους χρήστες κατά την αναζήτηση διαμοιρασμού εξοδολογίου.

4. Κάμερα, Σάρωση Αποδείξεων (OCR), QR ΑΑΔΕ & Φωνητική Καταχώρηση

  • Κάμερα (CAMERA) & Android Photo Picker: Χρησιμοποιούνται μόνο όταν επιλέγετε σάρωση απόδειξης ή QR. Η αναγνώριση κειμένου (OCR) και QR εκτελείται τοπικά στη συσκευή μέσω Google ML Kit· οι φωτογραφίες αποδείξεων δεν ανεβαίνουν σε servers του Finsay.
  • Επαλήθευση QR ΑΑΔΕ / Παρόχων Ηλεκτρονικής Τιμολόγησης: Όταν σαρώνετε ελληνικό φορολογικό QR, η εφαρμογή ενδέχεται να πραγματοποιήσει απευθείας κλήση ανάγνωσης (HTTPS) αυστηρά σε εγκεκριμένα domains (όπως aade.gr και πιστοποιημένους παρόχους ηλεκτρονικής τιμολόγησης) αποκλειστικά για ανάκτηση επωνυμίας εμπόρου, ΑΦΜ, ημερομηνίας και συνολικού ποσού.
  • Φωνητική Καταχώρηση (RECORD_AUDIO): Χρησιμοποιείται μόνο όταν πατάτε το κουμπί μικροφώνου. Βασίζεται στην ενσωματωμένη υπηρεσία SpeechRecognizer του Android (υπό τους όρους της συσκευής/Google) και το κείμενο αναλύεται τοπικά σε προσχέδιο. Το Finsay ΔΕΝ ηχογραφεί, ΔΕΝ αποθηκεύει και ΔΕΝ αποστέλλει αρχεία ήχου.

Το Finsay ΔΕΝ έχει πρόσβαση, ΔΕΝ συλλέγει και ΔΕΝ αποθηκεύει ποτέ βιομετρικά δεδομένα ή αποτυπώματα (πλήρης συμμόρφωση με GDPR Άρθρο 9 και νόμους ΗΠΑ όπως Illinois BIPA / Texas CUBI). Ο προαιρετικός 4-ψήφιος κωδικός PIN αποθηκεύεται αποκλειστικά τοπικά ως salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hash.
  • Έξυπνη Μνήμη Κατηγορίας Εμπόρων: Οι προτιμήσεις κατηγορίας ανά έμπορο/ΑΦΜ αποθηκεύονται μόνο τοπικά στη συσκευή (SharedPreferences).
  • Υπενθυμίσεις, Widget, Quick Tile & App Shortcuts: Οι άδειες τοπικών ειδοποιήσεων (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK, VIBRATE) χρησιμοποιούνται αποκλειστικά για τις υπενθυμίσεις σας και τις ειδοποιήσεις τοπικών προσχεδίων.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Scope & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy explains how Finsay (finsayapp.com, Android package com.kynigos.oikonomos) collects, uses, processes, stores, and protects your personal and financial tracking data. It is designed to comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR 2016/679), Greek Law 4624/2019, the UK GDPR, applicable United States privacy laws (including California's CCPA/CPRA, Virginia VCDPA, Colorado CPA, Connecticut CTDPA, and Texas TDPSA), and Google Play Developer Program Policies. Data Controller contact: support@finsayapp.com.

2. Categories of Data Collected & Private Profile Isolation

  • Account & Authentication Data: Name, email address, unique user identifier (UID), and profile photo obtained via Google Sign-In (Android Credential Manager) or Email/Password through Firebase Authentication.
  • Private Profile Details (Strictly Owner-Only): Optional fields such as phone number, date of birth, occupation, and city are isolated in a private sub-document (/users/{uid}/private/profile) readable and writable exclusively by your authenticated account—never exposed to other users during ledger-sharing lookups.
  • Financial & Budget Records: Amounts, titles/descriptions, categories, dates, payment sources, selected ledger currency, incomes, monthly budgets, recurring bills, savings goals, and member split/settle-up entries voluntarily entered by you.
  • Shared Household Data & Activity Logs: Member nicknames, roles (Creator, Editor, Registar), shared ledger links, and an activity log of ledger edits visible only to authorized members of that ledger.
  • Preferences & Local Settings: App language preference (all supported languages), theme mode, reminder schedules, and local merchant-to-category memory.

3. Bank & Digital Wallet Push Notification Reader (Optional, 100% On-Device)

Prominent Disclosure: The optional Bank Notification Auto-Catcher (Android NotificationListenerService) is disabled by default and operates 100% locally on your device.
  • Strictly Opt-In: Activated ONLY after you review our in-app Prominent Disclosure and manually grant Notification Access in Android System Settings.
  • Strict Filtering & On-Device Parsing: Finsay filters solely for recognized banking and digital wallet packages (ignoring personal messages, chats, emails, and OTP/2FA codes). Merchant name and transaction amount are extracted 100% locally in device memory.
  • Zero Transmission & Explicit Confirmation: Raw notification text is NEVER uploaded to Finsay servers, logged externally, or shared with third parties. Detected alerts create a local draft only; an expense is saved and synced to the cloud only if you actively tap Save/Confirm.
  • No Bank Credentials / No Open Banking: Finsay never requests or stores banking logins, passwords, PINs, card numbers, or PSD2/Open Banking API tokens.
  • Revocation: You may disable this feature anytime in App Settings or Android System Settings.

4. Camera, Receipt OCR, AADE / e-Invoicing QR Lookup & Voice Entry

  • Camera (CAMERA) & Android Photo Picker: Used solely when you choose to scan a receipt or QR code. OCR text recognition and barcode decoding run locally on-device via Google ML Kit; receipt images are not uploaded to Finsay servers.
  • Greek AADE / e-Invoicing QR Verification: When scanning a Greek tax/e-invoicing QR code, the app may perform a direct, read-only HTTPS lookup strictly to allowlisted official tax/e-invoicing domains (such as aade.gr and certified Greek e-invoicing providers) solely to retrieve merchant name, VAT/AFM, date, and gross total.
  • Voice Expense Entry (RECORD_AUDIO): Used only when you press the microphone button. Speech-to-text relies on Android's native SpeechRecognizer service (governed by your device/Google speech service terms), and resulting text is parsed locally into a draft expense. Finsay does NOT record, save, or upload audio files.

5. Biometric App Lock, Local Merchant Memory & Device Features

  • Optional Biometric & PIN App Lock: Fingerprint/Face Unlock uses Android BiometricPrompt handled exclusively by your device's secure OS hardware. Finsay NEVER accesses, collects, stores, or transmits biometric identifiers or templates (compliant with GDPR Art. 9 and US state biometric laws such as Illinois BIPA / Texas CUBI). Any optional 4-digit PIN is stored strictly on-device as a salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hash.
  • Smart Merchant Category Memory: Category preferences per merchant/VAT ID are stored strictly locally on-device (SharedPreferences).
  • Reminders, Widgets, Quick Tile & App Shortcuts: Local notification permissions (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK, VIBRATE) deliver only your configured reminders and local draft alerts.

6. Legal Bases (GDPR / UK GDPR), Zero Ads & No Data Selling

  • Performance of Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): Core budgeting, multi-currency tracking, analytics, cloud sync, shared ledgers, and data exports.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Mandatory acceptance of our Terms & Privacy Policy (version and timestamp recorded in your private owner-only profile), plus optional bank notification parsing, camera/OCR, microphone voice input, and reminder notifications (withdrawable anytime).
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Platform security, unauthorized-access prevention (Firebase Authentication & Zero-Trust Security Rules), and service reliability.
  • Zero Ads, No Data Brokers & No Third-Party AI Uploads: Finsay displays no ads, NEVER sells, rents, or shares personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising, and does not transmit your financial records to third-party cloud AI/LLM APIs.

7. Cloud Infrastructure, Security & International Transfers

Cloud data is hosted on Google Cloud / Firebase Firestore with TLS encryption in transit, AES encryption at rest, Firebase Authentication, and Zero-Trust Security Rules restricting reads/writes to authorized ledger members. Because Google Cloud / Firebase operates global infrastructure (including EU and US servers), any transfers outside the EEA or UK are safeguarded by EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, and Google LLC's certification under the EU-US / UK-US Data Privacy Framework. Optional Finsay Pro purchases are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing (we never see or store card numbers). Native Google Play In-App Review and In-App Update APIs may operate discreetly under Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Data Retention, Exports & Permanent Account Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as your account remains active or until you delete entries or your entire account. You may export expenses, incomes, balances, and member settle-up summaries locally to Excel (.xls), PDF (.pdf), or CSV (.csv) anytime. You can permanently delete your account, private profile, owned ledgers, and cloud/local data directly in the app (Menu (☰) → My Profile → Delete Account) or via our Account Deletion page. Residual cloud backups are purged within standard infrastructure cycles (up to 30 days).

  • Greece, EU/EEA & UK (GDPR / Law 4624/2019 / UK GDPR): You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability, object, withdraw consent anytime, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority—including the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (HDPA, www.dpa.gr) or the UK ICO.
  • United States (California CCPA/CPRA & US State Privacy Laws — VA, CO, CT, UT, TX, etc.): US residents have the right to know/access personal information collected, request deletion, request correction of inaccurate data, obtain a portable copy, and receive non-discriminatory treatment when exercising privacy rights. Finsay DOES NOT "sell" personal information and DOES NOT "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Sensitive personal information is used strictly as necessary to provide requested app features.
  • Exercising Your Rights: Use in-app tools or email support@finsayapp.com; we verify and respond within 30 days (or the statutory period under applicable law).

Finsay is not directed to children under 13 years of age (US COPPA) or under 16 years of age (or the applicable digital consent age in your EU/EEA/UK country under GDPR Art. 8).
